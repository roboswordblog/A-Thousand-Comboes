Long, long ago, the world was governed by a single measure of strength:

Combo Level.

A warrior's Combo Level determined how many attacks they could chain together without breaking their combo. The higher the Combo Level, the longer and more powerful their combos could become.

For generations, the maximum Combo Level anyone could reach was 1,000.

Those who mastered the art of chaining attacks became known as the Combo Knights. They were the strongest warriors in the world, and they protected the people from those who would use their power for evil.

At the height of the Combo Knights stood three legendary warriors.

Vaelen. Malakor. Morana.

All three had reached the legendary Combo Level 1,000.

Vaelen and Morana believed their strength should be used to protect the people and keep the world peaceful.

Malakor believed something very different.

He wanted to use his power to control the world.

The three eventually came into conflict over what the Combo Knights should become. After a heated argument, Malakor finally appeared to abandon his ambitions.

Or so everyone thought.

Months passed.

The world remained peaceful.

Until one day...

Morana disappeared.

Vaelen immediately suspected Malakor.

He confronted him and demanded to know where Morana had gone.

Malakor simply told Vaelen to follow him.

Vaelen followed him through a mysterious portal.

On the other side was a wasteland covered in volcanic ash. In the distance stood another portal—a gateway to something far more dangerous.

It led to a mysterious Rift in Time.

And on the other side of the Rift was Morana.

But before Vaelen could reach her, Malakor attacked.

Vaelen fought back, but Malakor had been hiding a terrible secret.

While everyone believed he had given up his ambitions, Malakor had secretly been training in the Dark Arts.

His power had grown beyond anything Vaelen had ever imagined.

Vaelen was defeated.

Malakor then used his newfound power to cast both Vaelen and Morana into the Rift, trapping them beyond the normal flow of time.

But Vaelen had one final trick.

Before he was completely exiled, he cast a powerful spell into the universe.

A spell that would travel thousands of light-years through space and time.

Its purpose was to find a warrior.

A warrior who could grow stronger.

A warrior who could master the art of the Combo.

A warrior who could one day reach the heights of the legendary Combo Knights.

A warrior who could save them all.

And now...

That time has come.

You are that warrior.

But the world you have entered is not the world Vaelen left behind.

Malakor has taken control.

Using the Dark Arts, he has placed a seal over the world's Combo Level, preventing ordinary warriors from progressing beyond Combo Level 750.

Only Malakor himself is exempt.

And there is another catch.

Those who manage to reach Combo Level 750 are taken by Malakor and made into his Royal Soldiers.

So you must become stronger—but reaching the highest level comes with a price.

You begin your journey with a low Combo Level.

Every battle gives you the chance to increase it.

Every new Combo Level allows you to chain more attacks together.

And somewhere beyond the Rift, Vaelen and Morana are waiting.

The path to becoming the warrior who can save them all begins with a single combo.

How high can you go?
